How do I approach designing software?

Working on this project helped me develop stronger design skills by learning how to break a large scene into smaller, single components. Before writing code, I planned the objects needed for my 3D scene and considered how they would fit together visually. I followed an iterative design process by creating basic shapes first, then refining them with transformations, and orientations. In later milestones I added camera controls, textures, and lighting. This approach allowed me to identify issues early and make gradual improvements throughout development. In future projects, I can apply these same planning and organizational techniques to create more complex software systems while reducing back tract and problem solving.


How do I approach developing programs?

One of the most valuable development strategies I learned was building and testing features incrementally. Rather than creating the entire scene at once, I implemented one object, texture, or lighting feature at a time and verified that each component worked towards the intention of scene before moving on. This was important to help with orientation  and adjustment too as it helped me improve picture scenario as a 3D render. Iteration played a significant role throughout the milestones, as each submission built upon previous work and feedback from my instructor. My approach evolved from focusing on functionality to emphasizing code organization, reusability, and visual fidelity. Throughout the project, I became more comfortable debugging graphics applications and solving issues related to transformations, textures, object placement, and rendering.


How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computational graphics and visualization have provided me with practical experience in mathematical concepts, spatial reasoning, and problem-solving. These skills support my personal or educational goals by strengthening my understanding of programming, computer graphics, and software development principles. Professionally, the project introduced me to technologies and techniques used in game development, simulation, visualization, and interactive applications. Learning how to design and render 3D environments has increased my confidence in working with graphics programming like OpenGL and has given me skills that can be applied to future software engineering, development opportunities, further learning with more professional APIs like Vulkan or personal projects.
